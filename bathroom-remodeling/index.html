What a remodel can include

Most projects start with what isn't working: a dated tub, a cramped shower, a vanity with no storage. From there the work can cover demolition, new tile on walls and floors, a new tub or walk-in shower, vanity and counters, lighting, trim and paint.

Smaller jobs are welcome too. Several of Tito's reviews are for tub and shower replacements.
