Every trade, one contractor.

A one-stop shop. Most remodels need a framer, a plumber, an electrician, a drywaller and a tile setter, each on their own schedule. Here, one contractor runs every phase, from the studs out.

01

Framing

New walls, moved walls, openings and the structure behind a new layout.

02

Plumbing

Supply and drain lines, tubs, showers, sinks and fixtures, moved where the new design needs them.

03

Lighting, outlets, fans and circuits for the new space.

Showers, tub surrounds, backsplashes and floors, with niches, benches and accent bands.

Questions

Before you call.

Do you offer free quotes?

Yes. Call 208-613-6837 or send the form below with a few details, and Tito will set up a time to look at the space.

What areas do you serve?

Twin Falls and the surrounding Magic Valley in southern Idaho.

What kind of work do you take on?

Complete remodels: bathrooms, kitchens, whole homes and commercial spaces, including the framing, plumbing and electrical. Bathrooms are a specialty. Flooring goes in as part of most remodels. The one thing we don't do is roofing.

Do you handle the plumbing, electrical and framing?

Yes. They're part of the remodel, so you don't have to hire and schedule separate trades. One contractor runs the whole job, from the studs out.

Do you take on commercial projects?

Yes. Commercial spaces get the same planning and finish work as a home remodel. Call or send the form with what you have in mind.

Who will be working on my project?

Tito Herrera, the owner, runs the job, and you deal with him directly.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 6 PM. Closed Saturday and Sunday.
