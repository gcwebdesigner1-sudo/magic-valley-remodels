The owner

Tito Herrera.

Tito started remodeling in 2003 and has worked across the Magic Valley ever since. Every job starts with a visit: he looks at the space, listens to what you want, and quotes the work before anything starts.

His reviews mention the same things over and over. He's honest, he communicates, and he calls himself a perfectionist for a reason. Magic Valley Remodels was nominated for Idaho's Best in 2026.

Questions

Before you call.

Do you offer free quotes?

Yes. Call 208-613-6837 or send the form below with a few details, and Tito will set up a time to look at the space.

What areas do you serve?

Twin Falls and the surrounding Magic Valley in southern Idaho.

What kind of work do you take on?

Mostly bathrooms: full remodels, walk-in and accessible showers, tub replacements, tile and vanities. Also kitchens and every phase of residential and commercial remodeling. Flooring goes in as part of most remodels. The one thing he doesn't do is roofing.

Do you take on commercial projects?

Yes. Commercial spaces get the same planning and finish work as a home remodel. Call or send the form with what you have in mind.

Who will be working on my project?

Tito Herrera, the owner, runs the job, and you deal with him directly.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 6 PM. Closed Saturday and Sunday.
